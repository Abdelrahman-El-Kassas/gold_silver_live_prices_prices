# Privacy Policy

**Gold & Silver Prices**

*Last updated: July 26, 2026*

## Introduction

Gold & Silver Prices ("we", "our", or "us") is a mobile application that displays real-time gold and silver prices. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

## Information We Collect

### We Do NOT Collect:
- Personal information (name, email, phone number)
- Location data
- Device identifiers
- Usage analytics
- Cookies or tracking data

### Third-Party Services

Our app fetches live metal prices from **Gold-API** (https://www.gold-api.com/). When you use the app, your device makes network requests to this service. Please refer to [Gold-API's Privacy Policy](https://www.gold-api.com/privacy) for information on how they handle data.

## Data Storage

We do not store any user data on our servers. All price data is fetched in real-time and displayed directly in the app without being saved.

## Internet Permission

The app requires internet access solely to fetch live gold and silver prices from the API. No personal data is transmitted.

## Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
